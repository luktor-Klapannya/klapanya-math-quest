## 2. 🌌 GLOBAL VISUAL DIRECTION
- **Theme Paradigm:** **Magical Academic Fantasy (แฟนตาซีวิชาการทรงเกียรติ)**.
- **Atmosphere:** Grand, enchanting, prestigious, immersive, and inspiring.
- **Aesthetic Guardrails:**
  - ✅ **Premium but Friendly:** Feels like a legendary magical school (Hogwarts meets fantasy academy), inviting exploration and pride of achievement.
  - ✅ **Child-Safe & Positive:** Bright highlights, crystalline luminescence, and uplifting magical effects.
  - ❌ **NOT Preschool / Cartoonish:** Avoid childish primary-color plastic bubbles or overly goofy mascot styles.
  - ❌ **NOT Dark Horror / Grimdark:** No skulls, blood, terrifying monsters, or oppressive gloomy darkness.
  - ❌ **NOT a Generic Corporate / Admin Dashboard:** Never regress to flat white SaaS admin panels, dull data tables, or generic corporate card layouts during gameplay.

## 3. 🥞 THE 7-LAYER RENDERING ARCHITECTURE
Every visual scene, gameplay screen, and battle arena in The Wisdom Academy strictly follows the locked 7-layer rendering hierarchy:

```text
 ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ LAYER 7: COMBAT & INTERACTION FX (Sparks, ice bursts, level-up glow)   │
 ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ LAYER 6: QUESTION & INTERACTION UI (Readable prompt, tactile options)  │
 ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ LAYER 5: HEADS-UP DISPLAY (HUD: Health, resolve, countdown timer, rank)│
 ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ LAYER 4: ENEMY / GUARDIAN / BOSS SPRITE (Frost Dummy, Dragon, Boss)   │
 ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ LAYER 3: SELECTED HERO / PROFESSOR SPRITE (Boy/Girl, Class, Tier L1–L4)│
 ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ LAYER 2: ATMOSPHERE OVERLAYS (Floating mist, snow dust, aurora glow)   │
 ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │ LAYER 1: STATIC BACKGROUND WORLD ART (Pure WebP/JPG scenic artwork)    │
 └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 6. ❄️ ARCTIC VISUAL SYSTEM (MATHEMATICS)
- **Primary Palette:** Bright Ice Blue (`#38BDF8`, `#7DD3FC`), Pure Snow White (`#F8FAFC`), Aurora Cyan (`#06B6D4`), Deep Midnight Slate (`#0F172A`).
- **Environmental Motifs:** Snow-covered pine forests, crystalline glaciers, frozen waterfalls, aurora borealis sky ribbons, and ancient ice shrines.
- **Rule:** The Arctic world must feel **crisp, magical, and luminous**—**never excessively dark, murky, or muddy**. High-contrast typography ensures crystal-clear readability.

## 7. 🎮 LEARNER GAMEPLAY & BRIGHT FROSTED ICE QUESTION PANEL
- **Immersive World First:** The battle scene renders real background scenery with the learner's chosen Hero facing opponents or guardians.
- **No Card-Heavy / Dashboard-Heavy Clutter:** Avoid floating dense grids of cards over the gameplay. Combatants stand directly on the world ground.
- **Bright Frosted Ice Question Panel (Normal Gameplay):**
  - **Visual Characteristics:** Snow white / very pale ice blue translucent frosted glass (`bg-white/85` or `bg-sky-50/90` with `backdrop-blur-md`).
  - **Typography & Readability:** Deep navy / royal-blue readable text (`text-slate-900` / `text-sky-950`) with maximum contrast.
  - **Borders & Lighting:** Subtle crystal border (`border-sky-200/80` or `border-cyan-300/60`), very light luminous shadow, and high tactile clarity.
  - **Rule:** **No dark heavy panels during normal Arctic gameplay.**
  - **Exception:** Dark/obsidian panels are strictly reserved for:
    1. Special Boss phase transitions
    2. Critical Danger / Low-Resolve states
    3. Cinematic warning overlays  
    *(Even in danger states, question text must remain 100% readable).*
- **Tactile Choices & Touch Targets:** Answer choice buttons provide $\ge 44\text{px} \times 44\text{px}$ touch targets with tactile press feedback (`active:scale-[0.98]`).
- **Mobile-First Baseline:** Formatted seamlessly for viewport widths from **$360\text{px}$ to $430\text{px}$** with zero horizontal overflow.

## 9. 🎓 PROFESSOR CONSOLE & TEACHER UI
- **Role Separation:** The Professor Console is a **dedicated mission-control screen**, completely separate from the learner's gameplay interface.
- **Professor Avatar Presentation:**
  - Uses compact, refined portrait icons in the top identity bar, session cards, and room header (`professor_male`, `professor_female`).
  - Full-body professor character art is reserved exclusively for Profile and Welcome screens.
  - **Rule:** Professors are **NEVER rendered as battle combatants or combat heroes**.
- **Desktop 3-Column Console:**
  - **Left (Span 4):** Live Classroom Roster (compact cards for 9–12 students: Avatar, Nickname, Live Status, Answer Checkmark, Accuracy, Streak).
  - **Center (Span 5):** Question Preview with Teacher's Secret Answer Key, Timer countdown, and Round controls (Start / Pause / Next).
  - **Right (Span 3):** Live Analytics (Class Accuracy gauge, Active Learners count, Hints Used, Recovery Queue, Shared Boss HP).
  - **Bottom:** Tactical Quick Actions bar (Send Hint, Extend Time, Pause/Resume, Boss Raid trigger, 1-Click Recovery Drill).
- **Mobile Professor View:** Single-column responsive layout with clear top switchers (`👥 นักเรียน`, `📝 โจทย์`, `📊 สถิติสด`).

## 10. ✍️ TYPOGRAPHY & FONT SYSTEM
- **English Display / Headings:** Elegant Fantasy Serif (`Cinzel`, `Cinzel Decorative`, `Georgia`, or system serif) with letter-spacing for grand headers.
- **Thai & Body Copy:** Clean, modern, accessible sans-serif (`Kanit`, `Prompt`, `Sarabun`, or system UI fonts) ensuring perfect clarity for mathematical formulas and complex word problems.
- **Rule:** Avoid thick, cartoonish, low-contrast, or overly decorative display fonts for question texts and numerical values.

---

## 11. 📱 RESPONSIVE & ACCESSIBILITY RULES
- **Viewport Hierarchy:** Mobile ($360\text{px} - 430\text{px}$) $\to$ Tablet ($768\text{px} - 1024\text{px}$) $\to$ Desktop ($1280\text{px} - 1920\text{px}$).
- **Touch Accessibility:** Every button, choice capsule, and interactive trigger must have a minimum touch target of **$\ge 44\text{px} \times 44\text{px}$**.
- **Safe Zones:** Keep interactive controls at least $16\text{px}$ away from viewport edges on mobile devices.
- **Zero Horizontal Overflow:** All screens must strictly adhere to `overflow-x: hidden` without clipped text or runaway elements.

---

## 12. 🖼️ ASSET PRODUCTION & OVERRIDE RULES
- **Raster World Backgrounds:** Approved PNG and WebP scenic backgrounds (`/public/backgrounds/...`) are the single source of truth.
- **Rule:** Do **NOT** vectorize or redraw approved world artwork into flat SVG graphics.
- **Decoupled Text:** Never bake Thai/English text, buttons, scores, or menu capsules into background artwork files. All UI elements must remain dynamic React overlays.
- **Layer Independence:** Heroes, Enemies, Bosses, and HUD layers must always remain independently animatable PNG/WebP assets with transparent backgrounds.
- **Rule of Asset Authority:** **APPROVED ART ASSETS OVERRIDE GENERIC DESIGN-SKILL SUGGESTIONS.** External tools/skills (such as UI UX Pro Max and Impeccable) may critique layout, accessibility, spacing, and usability, but must **NOT** replace or alter the approved Wisdom Academy fantasy art direction.

## 13. 🔍 QUALITY & AUDIT GATES (PRE-APPROVAL PROTOCOL)
Before any screen or UI modification is marked approved in this repository, it must pass this 4-step quality audit:

1. **`ui-ux-pro-max` Evaluation:** Validate style consistency, color harmony, typography pairing, and responsive balance.
2. **`impeccable critique`:** Review UX hierarchy, emotional resonance, and clarity of intent.
3. **`impeccable audit` & `polish`:** Check accessibility, spacing rhythm, touch targets ($\ge 44\text{px}$), and eliminate UI anti-patterns.
4. **Gameplay & Baseline Invariant Check:** Confirm that the **710Q Grand Campaign, Math Quest battle engine, scoring formulas, and Git baseline (`math-quest-v1.0`) remain 100% frozen and green (`245/245 passing tests`)**.
